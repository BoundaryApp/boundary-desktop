UX study · explainer text

Say less by default, explain on request, warn at the decision

About 130 explainer passages, roughly 2,350 words, sit in view across the app today. Most are accurate and some are needed, but a returning user reads past all of them to find the control. The comps below show how other apps split explanation between what is always shown, what is one hover away, and what appears only when the user is about to commit.

Recommendation. Keep at most one short line under a heading. Move reference text into an InfoTip (an ⓘ beside the label that opens on hover or click). Put how-tos behind a disclosure. Keep consequence text inline, but only where the user acts. Label state only when it differs from the default. Applied to the inventory, this removes about two thirds of the always-visible words without deleting any information. Details and phases are in spec.md.

InfoTip anatomy

A 14px circled i in --text-faint, 6px after the label. Hover for 400 ms shows the popover; click or Enter pins it; Esc or a click outside closes it and returns focus. The popover is 280px wide at most, --size-xs, about 60 words at most, with one optional link at the end. Explanations never go in a native title tooltip, which keyboard and screen-reader users can't reach reliably; title stays for names and the full text of truncated values.
