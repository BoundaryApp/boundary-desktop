The backup drive filled up on Thursday, two weeks earlier than the estimate. I traced most of it to the photo library. Every edit in the new editor writes a full copy of the original next to it, so a folder of 4,000 pictures had grown to 31 GB.

Turning off "keep originals" in the editor's settings stopped the growth. I deleted the duplicate copies by hand after checking that the edits still opened, which took most of an evening.

The estimate was wrong because I measured the library in March, before the editor update. I've added a monthly reminder to check the drive's free space. If it drops under 50 GB again I'll buy the larger drive rather than keep pruning.
