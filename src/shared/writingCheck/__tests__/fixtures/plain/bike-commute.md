I started riding to work in September. The route is 11 km each way, mostly on a canal path, with one bad junction near the station where cars turn across the bike lane.

The first month was hard. My legs were fine but I arrived too sweaty to sit in a meeting, so now I leave ten minutes earlier and ride slower. A rack and panniers replaced the backpack, which helped more than anything else.

Rain is the real test. I bought proper overtrousers after getting soaked twice in one week. On the worst days I still take the train, maybe once a fortnight, and I've stopped feeling guilty about it.
