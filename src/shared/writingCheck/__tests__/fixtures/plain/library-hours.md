The branch library on Elm Street will close at 6 pm instead of 8 pm on weekdays from the first of the month. The council cut the evening staff budget, and the library chose shorter hours over closing on Saturdays.

Saturday hours stay the same, 9 am to 4 pm. The book drop outside the main door stays open all the time, and returns are checked in each morning.

People who used the evening study room can book the room at the central library, which keeps its 9 pm closing. The bus from Elm Street takes about twenty minutes. The branch manager said the hours will be reviewed next spring, when the council sets its new budget.
