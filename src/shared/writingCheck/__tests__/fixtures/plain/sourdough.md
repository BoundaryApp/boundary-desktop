My starter stopped rising after I moved it to the shelf above the fridge. That shelf sits at about 18°C at night, and the starter likes 24°C or so.

I tried feeding it twice a day for a week. It bubbled a little but never doubled. Moving it back next to the oven fixed it within two days, so temperature was the whole problem, not the flour.

For the bread itself, the warmer starter cut the first rise from about seven hours to four. The crumb is more open now. I still overproof the second rise about one time in three, because I forget to set a timer. A kitchen timer by the bowl has worked better than my phone.
